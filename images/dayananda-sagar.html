Dayananda Sagar College of Engineering (DSCE)

Founded in 1979 under the aegis of the Mahatma Gandhi Vidya Peetha Trust, Dayananda Sagar College of Engineering (DSCE) is one of the most reputed and sought-after engineering institutions in South India. Known for academic excellence, innovative research, and strong industry integration, DSCE offers a platform for students to become future-ready professionals.

Located in Kumaraswamy Layout, Bengaluru, DSCE's central location and cutting-edge infrastructure make it an ideal choice for aspiring engineers.

Campus & Facilities

  • DSCE boasts a sprawling campus of over 30 acres with world-class amenities
  • Smart classrooms, advanced laboratories & R&D centers
  • Massive central library with over 100,000+ titles
  • Dedicated innovation & entrepreneurship cells
  • Hostels for boys and girls with modern amenities
  • Cafeterias, sports complexes, auditoriums, Wi-Fi campus
  • Tie-ups with foreign universities and industry partners

Placements at DSCE

  • Top recruiters: Infosys, Accenture, Wipro, TCS, Capgemini, Bosch, Dell, Cognizant
  • Highest CTC: Rs 28 LPA | Average CTC: Rs 5-6 LPA
  • Regular internship programs and industrial visits
  • Mock interviews, soft skills training & aptitude tests
  • Alumni network and career support beyond graduation

Admission Through Management Quota at DSCE

Eligibility Criteria

  • 10+2 or equivalent with Physics and Mathematics as compulsory subjects
  • Chemistry/Biology/Biotechnology/Computer Science as optional subjects
  • Minimum aggregate marks as specified by the college
  • Documents required: 10th & 12th mark sheets, ID proof, passport photos, etc.

Fee Structure

  • Management quota fee is higher than CET/COMEDK seats
  • Fee varies by department (e.g., CSE, ISE, ECE are premium branches)
  • Inclusive of tuition fee, development charges, and other applicable fees
  • Payment plans and installment options available
